ABOUT

Yudhanto Hendra P., ST

Architectural 3D Artist

I am an architectural 3D artist with an educational background in Architecture from Universitas Pancasila, Jakarta, Indonesia.

I have been working in the architectural 3D design industry for more than ten years, focusing on exterior and interior design and the development of minimalist spaces that emphasize both function and aesthetics.

My experience covers a wide range of projects, from residential to commercial developments. My educational background has provided me with a strong understanding of design principles and contemporary architectural styles.
